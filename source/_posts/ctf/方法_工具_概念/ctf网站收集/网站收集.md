# 工具

[jadx](https://github.com/skylot/jadx) 逆向，java反编译

[010editor](https://www.sweetscape.com/010editor/) 二进制浏览、编辑、结构体解析

[winhex](http://www.winhex.com/winhex/hex-editor.html) 与010editor差不多

[dnslog.cn](dnslog.cn) dns解析记录

[ceye.io](ceye.io) dns解析记录

[dirburst](https://sourceforge.net/projects/dirbuster/files/DirBuster%20Source/1.0-RC1/) 路径爆破

[ida pro](https://hex-rays.com/blog/) c++反编译

[postbin](https://www.toptal.com/developers/postbin/b/1690015955766-7669416554272) 请求调试

[binwalk](https://github.com/ReFirmLabs/binwalk) 隐写检测

[wireshark](https://www.wireshark.org/) 流量分析抓包工具

wsl2 window自带，linux子系统

[postman](https://www.postman.com/) 请求发送，当然这个一般直接用代码代替

xshell、mobxtm、putty ssh连接工具

winscp ftp连接工具

[beyond compare](https://www.beyondcomparepro.com/product/) 运维，路径比较

[frida](https://github.com/frida/frida) 逆向，程序调试

[breakautokey](https://github.com/autokey/autokey/) 简单加密算法autokey的爆破工具
